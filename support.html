Support

Help with CrewVest, Premium subscriptions, reports and encrypted backups.

Contact support

Email lapp.supportl@gmail.com. Include a clear description of the issue, your iOS version and your CrewVest app version.

Premium purchase or restore

Open CrewVest’s Premium screen and use Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Subscription billing and cancellation are managed in Apple Account subscription settings.

Encrypted backups

CrewVest cannot recover a forgotten backup passphrase. Keep the encrypted backup file and its passphrase in safe, separate places. Preview a backup before confirming a restore.

Privacy and safe support

Do not email passwords, backup passphrases, complete financial records or Apple Account credentials. CrewVest does not need those details to investigate a support request. See the Privacy Policy for information about support communications.
