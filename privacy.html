Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CrewVest, a developer based in Ukraine, handles information in the CrewVest iOS app and through support communications.

Information handled by the app

CrewVest lets you enter and manage financial and contract information. This may include contract and salary details, accounts and balances, expenses, bonuses, recurring payments, personal subscriptions, vacation plans, preferences and report content.

Local storage and processing

Your financial records and app preferences are stored and processed locally on your device. CrewVest does not require a CrewVest account and does not transmit these records to a CrewVest-operated server. CrewVest does not operate a cloud-sync service.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases and subscription management. The app receives product, transaction and entitlement information through Apple’s StoreKit services so it can determine whether Premium access is active. CrewVest does not receive your complete payment-card details or Apple Account password. Apple handles information under its own privacy policy and terms.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, CrewVest may schedule local reminders on your device. These reminders are not sent by a CrewVest server. You can change notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Exports and encrypted backups

When you choose to create a PDF report or encrypted backup, the app prepares it on your device. You choose whether and where to save or share the file. A destination you select, such as a files provider, email service or messaging app, may process the file under its own privacy policy. Keep exported files and backup passphrases secure. CrewVest cannot recover a forgotten backup passphrase.

Support communications

If you contact support, CrewVest receives the email address and information you choose to include in your message. Support communications are used to respond to your request, diagnose issues and maintain necessary business records. They are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or to meet legal obligations. Do not send passwords, backup passphrases, complete financial records or Apple Account credentials.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

CrewVest does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking. CrewVest does not sell personal information.

Data control and deletion

You control the records stored by the app. You can edit or delete records inside CrewVest. Removing the app from your device deletes its locally stored app container, subject to how iOS and any device backups you control operate. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription. To ask about or request deletion of a support communication, contact CrewVest at the address below.

Security

CrewVest is designed to keep financial records on your device and encrypt user-created backup files with the passphrase you provide. No method of storage is guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device, exported files and passphrases.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when CrewVest changes or legal requirements evolve. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the current version.
